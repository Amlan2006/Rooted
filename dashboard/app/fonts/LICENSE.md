`Geist-Variable.woff2` and `GeistMono-Variable.woff2` are the official variable builds from the `geist` npm package 1.9.2 (vercel/geist-font), subset with fonttools to Basic Latin, Latin-1 and common punctuation, arrows and math signs (kern, liga, calt and tnum kept). They are the same files the site ships.
